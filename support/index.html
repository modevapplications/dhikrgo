Prayer times look off

Open the Prayer screen and check your location and the calculation method. Choosing the method used by your local mosque or authority usually fixes small differences, and each prayer can be adjusted by a few minutes.

Reminders don’t arrive

Turn on Daily Reminders in the app’s Settings, and make sure notifications for Dhikr Go are allowed in the iPhone’s Settings app.

Moving to a new iPhone

Your counts, streaks, bookmarks and settings are part of your iPhone’s iCloud or computer backup. Restore the new iPhone from that backup and they come with you.

Is it free?

Yes, every part of the app is free. Tips are optional and unlock nothing. There are no ads and no account.
